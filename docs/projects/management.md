# Frog Management

[Back to my profile](../../README.md)

## Introduction

I'm building a staff-only application to replace workshop spreadsheets with one place for events, counter sales, tickets, queues, inventory and reporting.

## Problem

Connect the steps of running a workshop event so staff can work with the same ticket, order and stock information.

## My role

Building the staff interface, business workflows and Supabase-backed operations.

## Main features

- Event and product management
- Counter orders, tickets and queue display
- Inventory movements and event expenses
- Owner/staff access and financial reporting

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, React, Supabase, Recharts

These notes describe the concepts I am practising in the implementation.
